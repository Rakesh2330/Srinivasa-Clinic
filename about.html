Who She Is

A Legacy of Academic & Clinical Excellence

Dr. R. Vatsala is a highly respected dental specialist and academician with over three decades of clinical, academic, and research experience in Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics.

She completed her BDS from the prestigious JSS Dental College in 1992, followed by intensive House Surgency. Her academic journey began in 1992 as a Lecturer at KVG Dental College, Sullia, and later flourished at Farooquia Dental College, Mysore, teaching Prosthodontics, Conservative Dentistry, and Endodontics.

With an unwavering dedication to clinical perfection, she earned her MDS in Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics in 2016. Today, she serves as an Associate Professor, mentoring future dentists while bringing senior-level expertise to patients at Srinivasa Dental Clinic.
