Who He Is

A Legacy of Dental Excellence

Dr. P. Sheshadri is one of Mysuru's most respected dental specialists, with over three decades of clinical, academic, and research experience in Periodontics & Implantology.

He began his journey at JSS Dental College in 1987 and has since built an extraordinary career-from a young Lecturer to the Head of Department at Farooquia Dental College & Hospital. His expertise spans advanced dental implants, laser periodontics, and cortical implantology.

At Srinivasa Dental Clinic, Dr. Sheshadri brings world-class academic expertise directly to patient care_ensuring every patient receives the highest standard of dental treatment.

Professional Journey

Career Timeline

1993 – 2003

Lecturer – Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics

Joined Farooquia Dental College on 1st May 1993 as Lecturer in the Department of Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics. Served for 10 dedicated years, building strong clinical and teaching foundations.

2003 – 2006

MDS Course – Periodontics & Implantology

Enrolled in the MDS programme at JSS Dental College in August 2003, specialising in Periodontics & Implantology. Successfully completed the MDS degree in 2006.

2006 – 2009

Assistant Professor – Periodontics & Implantology, FDC

Joined Farooquia Dental College & Hospital as Assistant Professor in the Department of Periodontics & Implantology after completing MDS. Focused on academic teaching and clinical patient care.

2009 – 2014

Associate Professor – FDC

Promoted to Associate Professor in the Department of Periodontics & Implantology at Farooquia Dental College & Hospital, taking on greater responsibilities in academics, research, and clinical supervision.

2014 – 2023

Professor & Ph.D. Guide – FDC

Promoted to full Professor and recognised as a Ph.D. Guide, mentoring postgraduate dental students and contributing to research in periodontics and implantology at a national level.

2023 – Present

Head of Department – Periodontics & Implantology, FDC

Promoted as Head of the Department of Periodontics & Implantology at Farooquia Dental College & Hospital — leading clinical, academic, and administrative functions of the department.
