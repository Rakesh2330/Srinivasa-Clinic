Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our treatments, consultations, and post-operative care.

Are dental implants permanent?

Yes. With proper oral hygiene and regular dental checkups, titanium dental implants fuse directly into the jawbone (osseointegration) and can last a lifetime, functioning just like natural teeth.

Is dental implant surgery painful?

No. The procedure is performed under precision local anesthesia so you will feel no pain during treatment. Most patients report only mild tenderness following the procedure, which is easily managed with routine pain relief.

What are the clinic timings and consultation charges?

Our OPD runs Monday through Saturday from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM (Morning Session) and 4:30 PM to 9:00 PM (Evening Session). Sundays are fully closed (Weekly Holiday — No OPD or Emergency visits). You can schedule your consultation for Monday through Saturday by calling +91 94480 46136 or via our online booking form.

How often should I get my teeth cleaned and checked?

We recommend a professional cleaning and checkup every 6 months to detect any early signs of gum disease, cavities, or tartar accumulation before they require complex treatments.
