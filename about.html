Who We Are

Srinivasa Dental Clinic & Speciality Centre

Srinivasa Dental Clinic is a premier Speciality Centre located in the heart of Mysuru. We pride ourselves on offering advanced dental care using digital scanning and modern procedural techniques.

Our mission is to provide comprehensive dental solutions that blend cutting-edge technology with compassionate care. We understand that visiting the dentist can be stressful, which is why we've created a sterilized, hygienic, and welcoming environment for patients of all ages.

  • Certified Dental Specialists
  • 100% Sterile Environment
  • Advanced Diagnostic Tools
  • Patient-Centric Approach
